Sound effects

The wooden-door sound used in this build is by Kenney (kenney.nl), released under CC0 1.0, and converted from OGG to PCM WAV.

https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

“Keys on the table” by Areti18 (Freesound sound 394944), CC0 1.0.
The public HQ preview was converted to PCM WAV, normalized to -4 dBFS, and endpoint-faded.
https://freesound.org/people/Areti18/sounds/394944/

The heavy wooden-door close used in the playable build is Kenney’s doorClose_1 from RPG Audio, normalized to -4 dBFS.

“stapler.wav” by j1987 (Freesound sound 95566), CC0 1.0.
This real manual-stapler recording is used as foley for a manual ticket punch; it is not an on-location ticket-punch recording. The public HQ preview was trimmed to one action and normalized to -6 dBFS.
https://freesound.org/people/j1987/sounds/95566/

This build includes only the three credited effects: keys on a table, a wooden-door close, and stapler foley for a ticket punch. Earlier procedural paper, zipper, suitcase-wheel, key and ticket-punch prototype files are not included.
